## Rules

1. A repository being listed here does not constitute independent verification of its claims.
2. Runtime compatibility must be demonstrated; source aggregation is not runtime integration.
3. Quantitative claims require reproducible inputs, procedure, outputs, and analysis.
4. Security claims require appropriate security review; demos are not automatically production security.
5. Upstream/forked repositories must be labeled as such and must not be represented as original authored systems.
6. Historical material is preserved for provenance and must not be confused with present capability.
7. The strongest public path is: **flagship → evidence → federation → deeper archives**.

## Quality target

The goal is not to manufacture perfect scores. The goal is to make every repository **accurately scoped, reproducible, inspectable, professionally documented, and honest about what its evidence establishes**. That is the release criterion for this portfolio.
