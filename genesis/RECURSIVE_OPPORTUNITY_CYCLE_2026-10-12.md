## 6. Evidence-status vocabulary
- **DESIGNED:** specification exists.
- **ARTIFACT_PRESENT:** file or code exists at a versioned location.
- **TESTED:** a named test ran against an exact version.
- **OBSERVED_RUNTIME:** an operation was observed with a timestamp and trace.
- **INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED:** an independent party reproduced the result.
- **UNVERIFIED:** evidence is incomplete or unavailable.
